# Brand

## Logo

Use the uploaded logo asset (`public/logo/`) as-is. Never redesign,
recolor, distort, or regenerate it. Until the real logo files are provided,
use a text-based placeholder wordmark ("Firuta Tech Services" in Inter,
brand colors) — clearly a placeholder, not a fabricated logo mark.

Required exports once the real logo is supplied:

- Navbar (SVG, transparent background)
- Footer (SVG, works on navy background — likely a white/light variant)
- Favicon (`app/favicon.ico`, plus `icon.png` / `apple-icon.png`)
- Social preview / Open Graph image (1200×630)

## Colors

Navy `#0C355B`, Green `#5FAE45`, White, Gray. See `DESIGN.md` for full
token mapping and contrast rules.

## Tone of voice

Professional, engineering-led, technical, reliable. Write like an
engineering firm speaking to procurement/facilities managers — specific,
factual, no hype adjectives without a claim behind them ("high-efficiency"
needs a spec or standard reference, not just an adjective).

Never use cartoon graphics, meme-style content, or casual slang.
